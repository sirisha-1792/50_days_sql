# 50 Days SQL Challenge

## Projrct title
E-Commerce Sales Analysis System

## Project Objective

The main objective of this challenge is to strengthen practical SQL skills by working with realistic datasets and solving business-oriented analytical problems.

The project focuses on:

* Cleaning and transforming raw data
* Creating structured relational tables
* Establishing primary and foreign key relationships
* Analyzing customer behavior
* Analyzing product performance
* Measuring orders and sales trends
* Evaluating revenue performance
* Using advanced SQL techniques to generate business insights

---

## 💡 Business Insights

### Insight 1: Temporary Tables for Salary Analysis
Temporary tables can be used to calculate and store intermediate salary summaries, allowing businesses to analyze employee compensation without permanently modifying the database. This supports temporary workforce cost analysis and reporting.

### Insight 2: Structuring E-Commerce Data
Separating customers, orders, products, order details, and payments into individual tables creates a structured foundation for analyzing the complete customer purchase journey.

### Insight 3: Data Cleaning Improves Business Decisions
Cleaning duplicate records, standardizing text, trimming unnecessary spaces, and handling missing values improves data quality and ensures that business analysis is based on more reliable information.

### Insight 4: Relational Data Modeling Connects Business Entities
Applying appropriate data types, primary keys, and foreign keys creates reliable relationships between customers, orders, products, order details, and payments, enabling accurate multi-table business analysis.

### Insight 5: KPIs Measure Overall Business Performance
Calculating metrics such as total customers, total orders, total revenue, and average order value provides a high-level view of business performance and helps organizations track growth.

### Insight 6: Customer Analysis Identifies Valuable Customers
Analyzing customer orders, spending, repeat purchases, inactive customers, and customer acquisition trends helps businesses identify valuable customers and improve retention strategies.

### Insight 7: Product Analysis Supports Product Decisions
Analyzing product sales, revenue, pricing, and category performance helps businesses identify high-performing products and categories and make better inventory and product strategy decisions.

### Insight 8: Order Analysis Reveals Purchasing Patterns
Analyzing order frequency, order values, daily and monthly order trends, and order-size categories helps businesses understand customer purchasing behavior and identify periods of high demand.

### Insight 9: Revenue Analysis Identifies Growth Drivers
Analyzing revenue across different time periods, customers, products, and categories helps businesses identify the major contributors to revenue and understand sales performance.

### Insight 10: Advanced SQL Enables Customer Segmentation and Reporting
Using CTEs, window functions, ranking, `CASE` statements, and stored procedures enables businesses to rank customers, segment them by revenue, identify top performers, and create reusable analytical reports.
